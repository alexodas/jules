# Compact-mode crossing: development plan

The existing nine-level theorem and Palomar target remain the verified baseline.
The compact eigenvectors and their crossing are formalized. The complete
spectral-isolation and splitting certificate remains a research target.

For the same confined operator H = 1 + |p|² + Δ/16, consider the alternating
eight-site states on the rings generated by (1,0,0,0) and (1,1,0,0) under
(n₀,n₁,n₂,n₃) ↦ (-n₃,n₀,n₁,n₂). Let A be the first ring.

The eigenvalue branches for Hₜ = H + t P_A are 35/16 + t and
13/4 + √2. They meet at t* = 17/16 + √2, at energy E* = 13/4 + √2.

The research target adds three obligations: exact multiplicity two at E*,
an explicit gap to the rest of the infinite spectrum, and quantitative
splitting after increasing the weight of the edge from (1,0,0,0) to
(1,1,0,0). A stationary eigenvector is proved below. The compressed matrix
suggests an initial displacement ε/4 for the other level, but does not control
additional eigenvectors or coupling to the orthogonal complement.

## Progress and remaining obligations

- The radial-gap source proves the internal squared-norm bound < 2 and
  that an admitted vertex below squared radius 3 + 2√2 lies within radius 2.
  The latter reduces to 81 integer-coordinate cases.
- The same barrier strengthens the original nine-level response to
  coefficient 4√2 − 5 on 0 ≤ t ≤ 3 + 2√2, using the radius-two sector.
- The compact coordinate identities and natural-domain eigenvector
  equations, nonzero norms and orthogonality are formalized in
  `AmmannBeenkerCompactModes.lean` and `AmmannBeenkerCompactStates.lean`.
- `AmmannBeenkerCompactCrossing.lean` proves the crossing with two
  independent eigenvectors. Their nonzero sum remains at E* under every
  real edge-weight change ε. The unit difference (a − b)/4 has Rayleigh
  quotient E* + ε/4. An eigenvalue derivative or remainder estimate does
  not follow from this matrix element alone.
- Exact eigenspace dimension, spectral isolation, and the quantitative
  one-edge splitting certificate remain open.
- Product-zero conclusions should follow through the existing exact-divisor
  bridge after the new spectral conclusions have been proved.
- Prototype the independent Challenge before changing the Palomar target.
  Do not assume the gap or exact multiplicity that the theorem is meant to prove.

The ring intervention differs from the radius-two intervention in the
nine-level theorem; its lifting estimate cannot simply be reused.
The selected Palomar/Comparator statement remains the full-product theorem.

## Route to a spectral-isolation certificate

The radial barrier supplies an exterior onsite lower bound 4 + 2√2.
One route is a finite central-block estimate on the orthogonal complement
of the two compact states, combined with a bound on coupling to the exterior.
This requires a proved classification of the patch and a finite algebraic
certificate. Numerical finite-patch eigenvalues do not supply those proofs.

## Provenance

The ring patterns are the type-A and type-B states in M. Ö. Oktel,
Strictly localized states in the octagonal Ammann–Beenker quasicrystal,
Physical Review B 104, 014204 (2021), Section IV:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.08678 .

Local-symmetry constructions of tunable compact states are also established:
M. Röntgen, C. V. Morfonios and P. Schmelcher,
Compact localized states and flat bands from local symmetry partitioning,
Physical Review B 97, 035161 (2018):
https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.07806 .

The contribution to assess is the complete quantitative certificate for the
infinite confined operator, not rediscovery of compact states or the affine
transfer from eigenvalues to product zeros. Novelty and Palomar suitability
have not been established.
